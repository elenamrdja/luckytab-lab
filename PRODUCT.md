# Product Spec — LuckyTab Lab

## User
Consumers choosing everyday purchases and merchants looking for engaging, measurable promotions.

## Hypothesis
A probabilistic large reward can create more excitement/engagement than an economically equivalent guaranteed discount.

## Prototype
- Merchant configures reward budget and mechanism
- Consumer sees offer and simulated outcome
- System records exposure, choice, and response
- Dashboard compares expected cost, variance, conversion/engagement proxies

## Example mechanisms
- £5 guaranteed discount
- 10% chance of £50 reward
- Group jackpot
- Hidden random reward

## MVP scope
Simulation engine, experiment assignment, consumer UI, merchant dashboard, analytics.

## Out of scope
Real-money wagering, regulated gambling, payment processing, live merchant settlement.

## Success criteria
10 consumer tests, ideally 2 merchant conversations, and a clear comparison of behavioral appeal versus merchant economics.
